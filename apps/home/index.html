Updates 1.06
New

  1. New top reasons and comparison by week for credits app

  4. Contacts app with local list, quick actions, friend management, import friends as contacts, pinned contacts, user search

Why orion isn't mentioned in the offical client list:

OriginOS's RoturTW's metrics for rating clients simply do not help the amount innovation and fine detail orion implements. To me, innovation and the way of implementation is what matters and not the number of boxes checked. You won't see the Orion client in offical lists because no metric conveys orionclient better than actually trying it.
